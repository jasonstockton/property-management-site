Inquiry privacy notice

The inquiry form uses your name, email address, property location and type, and any details you provide to prepare a draft in your browser. If you open the draft, it passes those details to your email app. Oakridge receives the information when you send the email to hello@oakridgepm.com and uses it to respond to your inquiry.

DoorLoop is a separate service for rental listings and portal access. Information you enter there is handled through that service.
